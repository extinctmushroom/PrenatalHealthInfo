Why start early

The 90 days before conception matter most

An egg spends roughly three months maturing before it's ovulated. The habits you build now — blood-sugar stability, sleep, key nutrients — influence egg quality, hormone balance, and a healthy start to pregnancy. Folate is especially time-sensitive: the neural tube closes within the first 28 days, often before you know you're pregnant.
