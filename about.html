About

A calm, honest companion for the months before pregnancy

Willow exists to make trustworthy preconception information easy to find, understand, and act on — without the noise, fear, or unsourced claims that fill so much of this space.

How we source information

Every recommendation in the guide is tied to a numbered reference. We prioritize, in order:

  1. Major clinical bodies — the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG), the CDC, and the WHO.
  2. Government nutrition authorities — the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements fact sheets.
  3. Peer-reviewed research — randomized trials and reviews indexed in PubMed / PMC.

Where evidence is preliminary or mixed (for example, CoQ10 for egg quality), we say so plainly rather than overstating it. Guidelines change; we note that individual advice always belongs with your own clinician.

🔒 Your data & privacy

When Firebase is configured, your tracking data (workouts, supplements, notes) is stored privately under your own account and is only readable by you, enforced by Firestore security rules. If Firebase isn't set up, the dashboard runs in a local "demo mode" that keeps data only in your browser.

🛠️ How it's built

A fast, static site (HTML/CSS/vanilla JS) designed for GitHub Pages, with Firebase Authentication for accounts and Cloud Firestore for private, per-user tracking. No build step, no tracking pixels.

Important

Medical disclaimer

Willow provides general educational information about preconception health. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and using it does not create a clinician–patient relationship. It cannot account for your personal medical history, medications, or conditions.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting supplements, changing medications, or making significant lifestyle changes — particularly if you have a chronic condition (such as diabetes, thyroid disease, or PCOS), take prescription medication, or have had a prior pregnancy complication. If you have an urgent medical concern, contact your clinician or local emergency services.
